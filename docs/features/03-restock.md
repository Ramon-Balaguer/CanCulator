# Restock (A reponer)

Products whose stock is below their **minimum stock**.

- The **Comprado** (bought) button opens the add-lot form with the product already filled in.
- Shows the product photo.
- When nothing is below the minimum, the screen explains how to make a product appear: edit it and set «Stock mínimo» (minimum stock) to more than 0.

Related: [Adding lots and scanner](06-adding-lots-and-scanner.md), [Weight, volume and price](14-weight-volume-and-price.md).
