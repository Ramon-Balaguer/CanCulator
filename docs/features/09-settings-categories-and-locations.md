# Settings: categories and locations

In **Ajustes** (settings) you add, rename and remove categories and locations (for example, pantry or fridge).

- Changes are kept after closing and reopening the app.
- Categories and locations sync between phones. Each change creates an «editar» (edit) operation on the setting.
- Ajustes also contains: the language, phone sync, app installation, the version, the diagnostic pages, backup and reset.

Related: [Phone sync](10-phone-sync.md), [Backup and reset](11-backup-and-reset.md).
