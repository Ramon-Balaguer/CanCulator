# Statistics (Estadísticas)

Inventory totals and a table by category.

- Units and calories (kcal, rounded to whole numbers).
- Weight (grams) for solid products, litres for drinks, and volume for tomato, sauces and oils.
- Cost: price per unit multiplied by units. A product without price, weight or volume adds nothing to those totals.
- Numbers use thousands separators according to the language (4.380 in Spanish, 4,380 in English).
- The table fits narrow screens with horizontal scrolling.

Related: [Weight, volume and price](14-weight-volume-and-price.md), [Language](12-language.md).
