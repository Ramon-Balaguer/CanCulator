# Weight, volume and price

Optional fields on the product sheet. They are edited there and used in Statistics.

- **Weight (g)** for solid products.
- **Volume (ml)** for water and drinks, and also for tomato, sauces, oils and condiments. The form shows the field that matches the category.
- **Price (€)** per unit.
- They are filled in from a GS1 QR code (application identifiers 310n, 390n and 392n) or, for a barcode, the price comes from Open Prices.

Related: [Statistics](05-statistics.md), [ADR 0014](../adr/0014-volume-in-ml-for-drinks-and-sauces.md).
